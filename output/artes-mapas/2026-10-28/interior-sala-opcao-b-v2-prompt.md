Create one standalone interior background for the 2D RPG Droids Code. Input image 1 is MATERIAL/CRAFTSMANSHIP reference only: keep its oxidized patched metal, warm worn timber, restrained dusty ochre/rust palette, readable dark edges, small cool blue accent, crisp hand-painted game-asset detail. DISCARD its old layout, tall room proportions, bed, kitchen and overhead lamp. Input image 2 is the actual LPC protagonist sprite, used ONLY to guide believable consistent furniture scale; DO NOT DRAW ANY CHARACTER in the artwork. Use a compact landscape 4:3 rectangular room, roof entirely removed, classic top-down 2D RPG projection like Pokemon interior room maps: broad readable furniture tops, only short north-facing wall faces, axis-aligned floor edges, NO diagonal isometric diamond, NO perspective vanishing point, no view from human eye height. Furniture must show orientation in plan view and may face north/east/south/west according to use, not all face the viewer. Establish a single common tile scale: about 10 tiles wide by 7 tiles deep of floor; single chair footprint about 1 tile, square four-person table about 2 by 2 tiles; consistent chair sizes, generous one-person-wide walking gaps. Keep furnishings compact relative to room and avatar. One clearly open doorway centered on the SOUTH edge, connected to an unobstructed route to the conversation area; a small worn entrance mat may mark it. All other walls closed. Flat soft ambient light, small diffuse warm highlights, no ceiling visible and absolutely NO hanging lamp, bulb, ceiling fixture, suspended cables or foreground roof beams. NO BED, NO sleeping area, NO stove, NO kitchen, NO bathroom, NO stairs, NO technology terminal or protagonist puzzle. No people, silhouettes, avatar, text, signs, UI, logos, annotations, tiles drawn as measurement grid, or watermark. Give the image natural high-resolution finish without chunky oversized pixel blocks; preserve the salvage-town visual identity. Whole room cleanly framed with a narrow dark outside margin. It is a representative living/conversation room rather than a complete functional apartment. VARIANT B: a compact conversation lounge with an ASYMMETRICAL seating arrangement. A short repaired TWO-SEAT SOFA against the WEST wall faces EAST into the room, so it must be drawn in side/top orientation with its BACK along the west wall, not facing the screen. A single matching ARMCHAIR on the EAST side of a small low coffee table faces WEST toward that sofa. Both seats and sofa follow the SAME seat-width scale as the avatar and furniture; a two-seat sofa is two single seats wide, never enormous. The little low coffee table stands between them in the upper-middle room with sensible leg room, on a muted blue-grey rug. Put a small square wooden SIDE TABLE and a low storage cabinet along the upper-east wall, one tall narrow bookcase at the north wall, and only two modest plants at the room corners. No dining-table-and-four-chairs arrangement. Leave the bottom half and a route around the rug clear for a player walking in from the SOUTH doorway to talk to someone near the furniture. The upper room feels occupied and the entrance feels useful; avoid a huge empty hall. Domestic conversation space, useful readable footprint, not a bedroom or kitchen. Visually compare directly with another modest 4:3 room using the same materials, door size, camera and furniture scale. Show only this ONE complete room, not a collage.
