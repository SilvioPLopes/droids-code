Create one standalone interior background for the 2D RPG Droids Code. DISCARD its old layout, tall room proportions, bed, kitchen and overhead lamp. Input image 2 is the actual LPC protagonist sprite, used ONLY to guide believable consistent furniture scale; DO NOT DRAW ANY CHARACTER in the artwork. Use a compact landscape 4:3 rectangular room, roof entirely removed, classic top-down 2D RPG projection like Pokemon interior room maps: broad readable furniture tops, only short north-facing wall faces, axis-aligned floor edges, NO diagonal isometric diamond, NO perspective vanishing point, no view from human eye height. Furniture must show orientation in plan view and may face north/east/south/west according to use, not all face the viewer. Establish a single common tile scale: about 10 tiles wide by 7 tiles deep of floor; single chair footprint about 1 tile, square four-person table about 2 by 2 tiles; consistent chair sizes, generous one-person-wide walking gaps. Keep furnishings compact relative to room and avatar. One clearly open doorway centered on the SOUTH edge, connected to an unobstructed route to the conversation area; a small worn entrance mat may mark it. All other walls closed. Flat soft ambient light, small diffuse warm highlights, no ceiling visible and absolutely NO hanging lamp, bulb, ceiling fixture, suspended cables or foreground roof beams. NO BED, NO sleeping area, NO stove, NO kitchen, NO bathroom, NO stairs, NO technology terminal or protagonist puzzle. No people, silhouettes, avatar, text, signs, UI, logos, annotations, tiles drawn as measurement grid, or watermark. Give the image natural high-resolution finish without chunky oversized pixel blocks; preserve the salvage-town visual identity. Whole room cleanly framed with a narrow dark outside margin. It is a representative living/conversation room rather than a complete functional apartment. VARIANT A: a modest social/dining room organized around ONE square wooden table centered slightly north of the room midpoint on a muted faded blue rectangular rug. Exactly FOUR matching chairs placed on the north, south, east, west sides; each seat points toward the table, chair BACKS point away. Show the LEFT chair in side orientation facing right and the RIGHT chair facing left; the near chair's back is seen from behind, the far chair faces down. Leave small sensible gaps to sit and clear paths around the entire grouping. A low cupboard centered against the north wall, a small bookcase against the west wall, a small storage chest along the east wall and two modest plants at the room corners. Few everyday objects on furniture, no clutter on the floor. These are simple repaired wooden furnishings for a common house. Same scale and projection throughout.
